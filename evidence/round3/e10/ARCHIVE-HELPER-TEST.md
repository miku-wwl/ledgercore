The first PostgreSQL invocation failed because the child shell could not resolve `Get-FileHash`; it failed closed, left the WAL in `pg_wal`, and produced no archived segment. The helper now uses .NET `SHA256.Create()` and retains the same collision checks. No persistent cluster or unrelated database was accessed.

Resume only after the sharing-mode remediation is tested and its exact CI succeeds. Capture fresh post-fix counters and verify the complete chain before taking a backup.

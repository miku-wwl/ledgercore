# E3 performance and correctness findings

## Scope and evidence boundary

All E3 tests ran against the separate disposable PostgreSQL 18.7 database `ledgercore_e3_perf` on `127.0.0.1:55434`; the ordinary integration database and persistent service were not used. Source-under-test is `230e5873ba7f0d1c7df546eb1e81622fa12a8cc0` on `main`. The environment record includes the host, Java 21.0.2, PostgreSQL settings, Hikari pool size 10, and database isolation. The harness times real Spring service proxy transactions including commit and excludes HTTP.

The original hypotheses and workload JSON were committed before their runs and remain unchanged. H04 is explicitly a supplemental follow-up, registered after H03 exposed the one-organization limitation and before its 50,000-row fixture was added.

## Results at a glance

| Hypothesis | Verdict | Main observation |
|---|---|---|
| H01 — ledger history growth | INCONCLUSIVE | 100-to-100,000 median p50 rose 126.4× for balance SQL and 20.1× for transfer service; repeat spread at the largest size was about 2.3× and raw plans changed. |
| H02 — transfers under concurrency | SUPPORTED | Independent account pairs scaled to 1,094.59 operations/s at 50 clients; one hot payer reached 112.64 operations/s with 606.04 ms p95. All correctness counters were zero. |
| H03 — payroll, history, authorization plans | MIXED | Claim, history, and authorization plans used indexes in the saved dataset; 5,000-item payroll progress scanned the global result table. |
| H04 — cross-organization progress follow-up | SUPPORTED | Adding 50,000 unrelated results raised the target progress scan from 71,500 to 121,500 rows; target result counts stayed at 5,000. |

## Financial invariants

H02 and H03 recorded zero unexpected transfer failures, unbalanced entries, projection mismatches, negative ledger balances, receipt mismatches, duplicate idempotency keys, and opposing-direction deadlocks. H04's read-only post-fixture check repeated the five global financial checks with all zero results and confirmed no postings for its synthetic payer accounts. Original H03 invariant data is in `measurements/h03-payroll-query-plans-20261114T025611Z/global-financial-invariants.json`; the H04 check is in `measurements/h04-cross-org-progress-followup/post-fixture-invariants.log`.

## Engineering decisions

- No production balance or transfer write-path optimization was made. H01's large observed growth is a review candidate, but its pre-registered verdict is inconclusive due to plan variation and 100,000-row repeat spread. Any incremental-projection candidate needs a separate hypothesis and the full financial correctness gates.
- The batch-progress query is a measured global-scan concern. Its plan and row work are now isolated and reproducible; Round 2 should decide whether to change the query/index and add a multi-organization regression gate.
- No throughput result is a production capacity claim. The laptop, local storage/cache, 10-connection pool, and synthetic distributions materially constrain interpretation.
